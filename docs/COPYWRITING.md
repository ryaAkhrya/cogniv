# Cogniv - Copywriting & Content Draft

## Logo & Brand Guidelines
- **Nama Brand**: Cogniv (Cognitive + Vision/Security)
- **Bentuk Logo**: Simbol tameng (shield) minimalis dengan outline tipis. Di bagian tengahnya terdapat titik (node) yang memancarkan cahaya (glow) neon ungu atau biru. Filosofinya: Pertahanan yang didorong oleh kecerdasan buatan (AI).
- **Tagline**: Digital Safety First. AI-Powered Security.

## Hero Section
- **Headline (H1)**: Next-Gen AI Security for Enterprise Data.
- **Sub-headline**: Protect your infrastructure with real-time, AI-driven threat detection. Prevention is cheaper than recovery.
- **Primary Button**: Start Free Trial
- **Secondary Button**: View Pricing

## Features Section (The Glassmorphism Cards)
- **Feature 1**: Real-Time Threat Detection (AI monitors your traffic 24/7, catching anomalies before they breach).
- **Feature 2**: Automated Incident Response (Block malware and isolate threats instantly without human intervention).
- **Feature 3**: Zero-Trust Architecture (Verify every request, trust no one. Absolute data integrity).

## Pricing Section (The Toggle Showcase)
- **Starter ($49/mo)**: Basic AI monitoring, up to 10 endpoints, 7-day log retention.
- **Pro ($199/mo)**: Advanced heuristics, unlimited endpoints, priority support, 30-day log retention.
- **Enterprise (Custom)**: Dedicated AI model, on-premise deployment, 24/7 dedicated security team.
